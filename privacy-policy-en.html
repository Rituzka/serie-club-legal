Serie Club Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Serie Club ("the app") is a free application for discovering and tracking TV series. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is treated.

Summary

Serie Club does not require an account or sign-in, shows no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell or share information with third parties. Almost all information generated by your use of the app is stored only on your own device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, using your phone's own storage (not on any server):

  • Your selected region (e.g. Spain, Argentina, or Finland)
  • Your selected language
  • The list of series you added to "My Series"
  • Internal identifiers for scheduled local notifications, so they can be canceled if you remove a series

This information never leaves your device except in the cases described below. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed along with it.

Third-party data (TMDB)

To show you information about TV series (titles, images, synopses, available streaming platforms, release dates), the app queries the public API of The Movie Database (TMDB). These queries only include the series identifier or the text you search for — no personal information or account identifiers of yours. TMDB has its own privacy policy, available on their site.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your own device to let you know when a new episode of a series you follow is released. These notifications are handled by your phone's operating system; they are not sent through any external server or third-party push notification service.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect your location
  • It does not use analytics, usage tracking, or advertising trackers
  • It does not show ads
  • It does not require or collect your email, name, or other identifying information
  • It does not share or sell information to third parties

Children

Serie Club is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if an analytics service or user account feature is added in the future), this same page will be updated with a new update date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, you can reach us at serieclub.official@gmail.com.
